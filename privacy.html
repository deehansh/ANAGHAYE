Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

ANAGHAYE AI respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you use our website and its AI-powered tools.

1. Information We Process

Different tools process information in different ways:

  • Browser-based tools: Tools such as the word counter, text cleaner, calculators and other utilities generally process the information you enter directly in your browser.
  • AI-powered tools: When you use AI Chat, Writing Studio, Translator, Coding Assistant, PDF Assistant or other connected AI features, the content you submit may be sent to our Cloudflare Worker backend and the relevant AI service to generate a result.
  • AI Image Studio: Your image-generation prompt may be sent to the configured AI image service.
  • Technical information: Our backend uses an IP-based daily usage limit. Technical information needed to operate and protect the service may therefore be processed.

2. PDF Assistant

PDF text is extracted in your browser using PDF.js. The extracted text, rather than the original PDF file, is sent to the AI backend when you request an AI summary. Scanned or image-only PDFs may not work because OCR is not currently included.

3. How We Use Information

Information processed through the website may be used to:

  • Provide the requested tool results.
  • Operate and maintain the website and its backend.
  • Apply usage limits and help prevent misuse.
  • Maintain the security and reliability of the service.

4. Third-Party Services

ANAGHAYE AI uses Cloudflare Workers and Cloudflare Workers AI for backend and AI functionality. Information submitted through connected AI tools may be processed by these services under their applicable terms and privacy policies.

Third-party services may process technical information according to their own policies. Please review their privacy documentation before using the AI features.

5. Data Storage and Retention

The website does not currently provide user accounts, personal saved history, payment processing or cloud file storage. However, backend usage records and technical logs may be processed or retained by the infrastructure providers according to the deployed configuration and their applicable policies.

We do not make a promise that submitted AI content is immediately deleted from every service or provider system.

6. Cookies and Analytics

ANAGHAYE AI does not currently include its own analytics system or advertising tracking system. However, hosting or infrastructure providers may use necessary technical mechanisms to operate and secure their services.

7. Text-to-Speech

Text-to-Speech uses speech synthesis available in your browser or device. Voice availability and processing may depend on your browser and operating system.

8. Children's Privacy

ANAGHAYE AI is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. Users should not submit children's personal or sensitive information through AI tools.

9. Security

We take reasonable steps to operate the website securely. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Your Responsibility

Please do not submit passwords, financial details, medical records, government identification numbers, confidential business information or other sensitive personal information to our AI tools.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website, features or services change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.

12. Contact

For privacy-related questions, please contact the ANAGHAYE AI website operator using the official contact details published on our website.
